# Video Slim Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Video Slim accesses videos that you allow on your iPhone so it can compress
videos and help organize your photo library.

## Data collection

The developer does not collect personal information, videos, photos, usage
history, device identifiers, file names, or location data. The app contains no
advertising SDK, tracking SDK, analytics SDK, or developer account system.

## Videos and the photo library

Video Slim uses Apple's photo library APIs to list and preview videos, create
and save compressed copies, and delete an original only when you explicitly
choose to do so. Compression runs on your iPhone. Videos are never sent to a
developer-operated server.

A video stored only in iCloud Photos may be downloaded from Apple's services
when you choose to preview or compress it. This network activity is provided by
Apple's photo library services.

## Deleting originals

Original videos are never deleted automatically. The delete option is shown
only after the compressed copy has been saved and verified as playable.
Deletion requires your explicit action and confirmation in iOS. When iCloud
Photos is enabled, deletion also applies to other devices using the same Apple
Account.

## Information stored on the device

Video Slim stores the number of successful compressions, the exact cumulative
space saved, migration flags from older versions, and the last app version that
requested a rating in UserDefaults on your iPhone. This information is not sent
to the developer. Pro entitlement is checked through Apple's StoreKit. Apple's
terms and privacy policy apply to purchases.

## Third parties

Because the developer does not collect user data, the developer does not sell
or provide that data to third parties.

## Contact

Use the [support request form](https://github.com/somtum1120/video-slim-support/issues/new).
Do not attach personal information or the video itself.

## Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its last-updated date will be revised.
